About SmartLoanCalc

SmartLoanCalc is a free mortgage payment calculator. It estimates your monthly principal and interest, property tax, home insurance, PMI and HOA fees, and shows a year-by-year amortization schedule. Everything is calculated in your browser. We never ask for your email or phone number, and the numbers you type are not sent to us.

What the calculator includes

  • Principal and interest: the standard fixed-rate formula M = P·r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of payments.
  • Taxes, insurance and HOA: enter annual tax and insurance and monthly HOA dues. These vary a lot by location, so use your own quotes.
  • PMI estimate: when your down payment is under 20%, we add an estimate of 0.5% of the loan per year. Real PMI commonly ranges from about 0.3% to over 1% depending on credit score and down payment.
  • Amortization schedule: how much principal and interest you pay each year and the balance that remains.

A worked example

A $400,000 loan at 6.5% over 30 years has a principal-and-interest payment of about $2,528. In month one, about $2,167 goes to interest and about $361 to principal. By around year 15 the split is roughly $1,630 interest and $900 principal. Early payments are mostly interest, which is why extra principal payments early in the loan have such a large effect.

Why a payment estimate is not the whole cost

Listing sites often show only principal and interest. Property tax, insurance, PMI and HOA fees can add hundreds of dollars a month, so enter them before deciding what you can afford. Compare Loan Estimates from at least three lenders; the lender must provide one within three business days of your application.
