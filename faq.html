Mortgage FAQ

Short, sourced answers to common home-financing questions.

Using SmartLoanCalc

Is the calculator accurate?

It uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. Results are estimates because your real tax, insurance, PMI and fees will differ. Report any math error through the contact page.

Do you collect my numbers or ask for my email?

No. Calculations run in your browser and the inputs are not sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for cookies used by analytics and advertising.

Does it include taxes and insurance?

Yes. Click "Add Taxes, Insurance & HOA" to enter them. PMI is estimated at 0.5% of the loan per year when your down payment is under 20%.

Down payments and PMI

Do I need 20% down?

No. Many loans allow less (conventional loans from 3%, FHA from 3.5%), but under 20% you generally pay PMI or mortgage insurance. Balance a larger down payment against keeping an emergency fund.

How do I get rid of PMI?

On conventional loans you can usually request cancellation when the balance reaches 80% of the original home value, and it ends automatically at 78% if you are current. FHA mortgage insurance follows different rules; ask your servicer.

Rates, lenders and credit

How do I compare lenders?

Request Loan Estimates from at least three lenders on the same day with the same loan type. Compare the rate, origination charges and total closing costs. The lender must send a Loan Estimate within three business days of your application.

15-year or 30-year?

A 15-year loan has a higher payment but much less total interest and usually a lower rate. A 30-year loan has a lower required payment and more flexibility. Choose based on your budget and how stable your income is.

What credit score do I need?

Conventional loans commonly need about 620 and FHA loans can allow 580 with 3.5% down. Higher scores, especially 740 and above, generally qualify for better rates.

Costs

What are closing costs?

Fees for the loan and transfer, such as lender charges, appraisal, title and prepaid taxes and insurance. They are commonly estimated at 2% to 5% of the price. Your Loan Estimate itemizes them, and you receive a Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing.

What is escrow and why can my payment change?

Your servicer may collect property tax and insurance monthly and pay the bills for you. If those bills rise, your payment rises, even with a fixed-rate loan.

What is amortization?

The schedule of how each payment splits between interest and principal. Early payments are mostly interest. Open the schedule in the calculator to see your own loan.

Final steps

What if the appraisal comes in low?

The lender bases the loan on the lower value. You can renegotiate the price, pay the gap in cash, challenge the appraisal, or walk away if your contract allows.

Can I pay extra principal?

Usually yes, but check for a prepayment penalty. Extra principal lowers the balance and total interest. Make sure extra payments are applied to principal.
