Privacy Policy

How SmartLoanCalc handles your information, cookies and advertising.

Introduction

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains what information SmartLoanCalc (“we,” “us”) collects when you visit smartloancalc.online, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site. We try to collect as little personal information as we can.

Information you give us

The calculators on this site run in your browser. The loan amounts, rates and other numbers you enter are used to show results on your screen and are not sent to our servers or stored by us. If you contact us through the contact form or by email, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address and message. We use this only to read and respond to your message and keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list without your consent.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, our hosting provider and the tools we use may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, language, the pages you view, the time and date of your visit and the website that referred you. This information helps us keep the site secure, understand how it is used and fix problems. The site also loads some resources, such as scripts for the payment chart, from third-party content delivery networks, which means those providers can see your IP address when your browser requests the files.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files placed on your device. We and our partners use them for these purposes:

  • Essential cookies that make the site work and remember basic settings.
  • Analytics cookies that tell us how visitors use the site.
  • Advertising cookies used by Google and other partners to show and measure ads.

You can control cookies in your browser settings, delete cookies already stored and block cookies from particular sites. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser. We use the reports to improve our content. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense and advertising

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices and where you live.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not control the cookies set by third parties, and their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we show a consent message so you can accept or reject non-essential cookies and personalized advertising before they are used. You can change your choice at any time through that message or your browser settings. Where data protection law applies, our legal bases for processing are your consent for advertising and analytics cookies and our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure and working.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to know what we hold, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent and to receive a copy of your data. Residents of some US states, including California, may also have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. To make a request, email support@smartloancalc.online. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by law.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve the site and calculators.
  • To understand which content is useful and to plan new guides.
  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To display and measure advertising through our advertising partners.
  • To meet legal obligations and prevent misuse.

We do not use your information to make decisions that significantly affect you, and we do not build profiles about you ourselves.

Sharing information

We share information only as described here. Service providers such as our hosting provider, analytics provider and advertising partners process information for us under their own terms. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users. We do not sell your contact details to lenders, brokers or marketers.

Data retention and security

We keep messages you send for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable time afterward, then delete them. Technical logs are kept by our providers for limited periods under their policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, such as encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive data such as Social Security or bank account numbers through the contact form or email.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. When we transfer personal information, we rely on the safeguards our providers use, such as contractual protections, where the law requires them.

Children’s privacy

The site is intended for adults who are considering a home purchase and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to third-party websites such as government and educational sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Changes to this policy and contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date at the top when we do. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy. If you have questions about this policy or about your information, contact us at support@smartloancalc.online or through the contact page.

Do Not Track and browser signals

Some browsers send signals such as Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. There is no single industry standard for Do Not Track, so the site may not respond to it. Where a consent or advertising tool supports Global Privacy Control, it may honor that signal according to its own terms and applicable law. You can also use your browser’s privacy settings, private browsing mode or extensions that block trackers.

Examples of what we do not collect

We do not ask for, and do not want, your Social Security number, driver’s license number, bank account or card numbers, passwords or loan application details. The calculators do not require an account, and we do not sell loan leads. If you ever see a request that appears to come from us asking for such information, treat it as suspicious and report it to us.
