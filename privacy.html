Privacy Policy (No Legal Jargon)

Last Updated: November 2026. I promise this is the easiest Privacy Policy you will ever read. No confusing legal terms, just the honest truth about data.

Let’s be honest with each other. Nobody reads Privacy Policies. They are usually long, boring walls of legal text written by corporate lawyers to protect massive companies from lawsuits. They are designed to be confusing.

But I am not a massive company. I am just a guy who built a mortgage calculator to help people avoid getting ripped off. And I believe that you have a right to know exactly what happens to your data when you visit my website.

So, I am writing this in plain English. If you have any questions about this policy, you can always email me at support@smartloancalc.online.

1. The Core Philosophy: Your Data is Yours

I hate spam. I hate robocalls. I hate opening my inbox and seeing 50 emails from companies I never signed up for. I know exactly how frustrating it is, especially when you are dealing with something as stressful as a mortgage.

Because of this, I made a hard rule when I built SmartLoanCalc: We will never ask for your personal information just so you can use our calculator.

You don't need to create an account. You don't need to give me your email address. You don't need to enter your phone number. You don't even need to give me your real name. You can use the calculator entirely anonymously. We believe in privacy by default.

2. What Happens to the Numbers You Put in the Calculator?

This is the most important part. When you type in your home price, your down payment, your income, and your credit score, none of that data leaves your computer.

Our calculator is built using JavaScript. This means the math happens entirely inside your web browser. Your device is doing the work. The numbers you enter are never transmitted to our servers, they are never stored in a database, and they are never sold to third parties.

Even if I wanted to see your numbers (which I don't), I couldn't. They are invisible to me. Once you close the browser tab, that information vanishes forever. It is safe, and it is private.

"I don't want to know how much money you make, what your credit score is, or how much you have saved for a down payment. That is your private business. I just want to help you do the math."

3. What Data DO We Collect? (The Boring Tech Stuff)

While we don't collect the financial data you put in the calculator, we do use a few standard web tools that collect non-personal information. This is standard for almost every website on the internet.

A. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find our site and how they use it. For example, it tells us "1,000 people visited the homepage today, and 200 of them read the PMI article."

Google Analytics collects things like:

  • Your IP address (a unique number assigned to your internet connection).
  • The type of device and browser you are using (e.g., iPhone, Safari).
  • The pages you visit on our site and how long you stay.
  • The website you came from (e.g., a Google search, or a link on Facebook).

Why do we use this? Because it helps me figure out which articles are helpful and which ones are boring. It helps me make the website better. It does not tell me who you are. It just tells me general patterns.

B. Cookies

Cookies are tiny text files that a website puts on your computer. They are not viruses, and they don't scan your hard drive. They just help websites remember things (like if you are logged in, or what your language preferences are).

You can turn off cookies in your browser settings at any time. Just know that if you do, some features on the internet (not just our site) might not work perfectly.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense (How We Keep This Free)

I need to be transparent with you. Building and hosting a website costs money. To keep SmartLoanCalc 100% free for everyone, we display advertisements.

We use Google AdSense to serve these ads. Here is how it works:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • The DoubleClick Cookie: Google uses a cookie called the DoubleClick cookie, which enables it to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • Your Control: You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.

Essentially, Google tries to show you ads for things you actually like. If you have been searching for cars, you might see an ad for a car. It is how the modern internet survives. We don't control the ads, and we don't know which ads you see. If you have an issue with an ad, you have to report it to Google.

A Note on Data Selling: I know many people are worried that websites are "selling their data." I want to be very clear: SmartLoanCalc does not sell your personal information to anyone. We don't have a list of names or emails to sell, because we never ask for them in the first place.

5. Links to Other Websites

Sometimes, I will link to other websites that have helpful information. For example, if I write an article about credit scores, I might link to the official government website where you can check your score for free.

When you click a link and leave our site, you are now on a different website, and our Privacy Policy no longer applies. You need to read their Privacy Policy to see how they handle your data. We have no control over what other websites do. We just hope they are as honest as we are.

6. Data Security: Why You Are Safe Here

Because our calculator runs entirely in your browser, the risk of your financial data being hacked is essentially zero. There is nothing to hack because we don't store any of it.

As for your general browsing data (like your IP address), we take reasonable security measures to keep our website safe and secure. We use HTTPS encryption (that little padlock icon in your browser) which means that the connection between your computer and our website is scrambled and protected from hackers.

7. Your Rights (The Legal Section)

Depending on where you live in the world, you may have specific legal rights regarding your data. For example, if you live in Europe, you have rights under GDPR. If you live in California, you have rights under CCPA.

Even though we don't collect much data, here are your rights in plain English:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to ask if we have any of your data (we won't).
  • Right to Correction: If you think we have wrong data about you, you can ask us to fix it.
  • Right to Deletion: You can ask us to delete any data we have about you.
  • Right to Object: You can object to us using your data for certain purposes.

If you want to exercise any of these rights, just send an email to support@smartloancalc.online and I will do my best to help.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for adults who are looking to buy a home. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent and you discover that your child has provided information to us (somehow, though I don't know how they could), please contact us, and we will remove it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As the website grows and changes, or as the law changes, I might have to update this Privacy Policy. When I do, I will change the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. I will not send you an email to tell you about the changes (because I don't have your email address!). So, if you are interested, just check back on this page every once in a while.
