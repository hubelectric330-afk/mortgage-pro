Contact Us

Send a question, correction or feedback.

We are glad you are here. SmartLoanCalc is an independent educational site, and reader feedback is one of the most useful things we receive. You can write to us at support@smartloancalc.online or use the form below. We read every message, and we aim to reply within a few business days, although busy periods can take longer. This page explains what to include so we can help quickly, what we can and cannot help with, and how we handle your message.

Send Us a Message

Have a question or feedback? Fill out the form below.

What we can help with

  • Calculator questions. If a result looks wrong or confusing, tell us the inputs you used and the result you expected.
  • Corrections. If you find a mistake in a guide, a number that does not add up or an outdated rule, we want to fix it.
  • Feedback and suggestions. Tell us what was hard to follow or which topic you would like us to cover next.
  • Technical problems. Pages that do not load, layout problems on your phone or features that stop working.
  • Privacy requests. Questions about your information or requests to access or delete it (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Business and press. Partnership, media or advertising inquiries.

What we cannot help with

SmartLoanCalc is not a lender, broker or financial adviser. We cannot approve a loan, tell you which rate you will be offered, review your loan documents, give legal or tax advice or recommend a specific lender. For those needs, speak with a licensed loan officer, a real estate attorney, a tax professional or a HUD-approved housing counselor. We are happy to point you to the relevant guide on our site or to an official resource such as the CFPB.

How to write a message we can act on

A clear message gets a faster and better answer. Whenever possible, include the following.

  • The page address or title where you saw the problem.
  • The numbers you entered, such as price, down payment, rate, term, taxes and insurance.
  • The result you got and the result you expected, and how you worked out the expected figure.
  • Your device and browser if the problem is technical.
  • A way to reach you, such as your email address.

Please do not include your Social Security number, bank account details, card numbers, passwords or loan application documents. We never need them, and email is not a secure way to send them.

What happens after you write

Your message goes to the site owner. We read it, check the issue and reply to the email address you provided. If you reported a calculation or content error, we verify it against the formula and the sources, correct the page if needed and update the review date. If we cannot reproduce a problem, we may ask for more detail. We use your name and email only to respond. See our Privacy Policy for how contact information is handled and how long we keep it.

Reporting an error in a guide or the calculator

Accuracy matters on a financial site. If you believe a figure is wrong, please tell us. The most helpful reports include the page, the specific number, your own calculation and the source you used, for example a spreadsheet PMT function or an official publication. We compare it with the formula described on our How It Works page. If you are right, we fix it, and if the difference comes from a different assumption, we may add a note so other readers are not confused.

Advertising, partnerships and media

We welcome questions from journalists and from organizations that want to discuss legitimate educational partnerships. We do not sell recommendations, we do not publish paid placements disguised as advice and we do not accept payment to favor a lender or product. Any sponsored content in the future would be clearly labeled. If you represent an organization, tell us who you are and what you propose so we can judge whether it fits our readers.

Beware of scams

Mortgage and home buying are common targets for fraud. We will never ask for your Social Security number, bank details or payment to use the site, and we do not send unsolicited loan offers. Be cautious about emails or messages that claim to be from us and ask for money or sensitive information. If you receive one, do not respond, and tell us. When you are buying a home, always confirm wire instructions by calling a phone number you already know, and be careful about unsolicited loan offers (see what happens at closing).

Before you write: quick answers

Many questions are already answered. Check the FAQ for short answers on accuracy, PMI, closing costs, escrow and extra payments. Read How It Works for the formulas and test cases, and browse the guides for in-depth explanations. If your question is still open after that, we would like to hear it.

Response times and expectations

We are a small independent site. We try to reply within a few business days, but we cannot promise a specific response time, and we may not be able to answer every message individually. Urgent financial or legal decisions should not wait for our reply. Contact your lender, attorney or housing counselor for time-sensitive matters.

Accessibility

If you have trouble reading or using any page, for instance with a screen reader, text size or contrast, tell us what you were trying to do and which device or assistive technology you use. We will look for a fix.

Thank you

Every correction, question and suggestion makes the site better for the next reader. Thank you for taking the time to write. Please also read our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Why a calculator result may differ from your lender’s

Before you write, it helps to know the usual reasons for a difference: a different interest rate, mortgage insurance, escrow for taxes and insurance, discount points, fees rolled into the loan amount, a different term or a different payment date. If your lender’s figure is higher, ask for an itemized breakdown and compare each line with the calculator’s breakdown. If you still cannot explain the gap, send us both sets of numbers and we will help you look for the cause.

How we use your feedback

We review messages regularly. Corrections are made first. Suggestions are collected, and when several readers ask for the same thing, such as a new calculator option or a guide on a topic, it moves up our list. We may quote an anonymous question or idea in a guide or FAQ answer to help others, but we will not publish your name or email without asking you first.

Tips for first-time contact

If this is your first message, a short, specific note works best. One question per message is easier to answer than several, and screenshots are welcome if they do not show private information such as account numbers. If you are writing because a number looks wrong, tell us how you checked it, since that helps us find the cause quickly.

Other ways to reach us

The fastest way to reach us is email at support@smartloancalc.online. The form on this page opens your email program, so if nothing happens when you press send, copy our address and write to us directly. We do not take phone calls or offer live chat, and we do not accept loan applications through this site.
