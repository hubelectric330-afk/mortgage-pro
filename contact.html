What I CANNOT Help You With (Important)

I need to be very clear about this, because AdSense policies and ethics require it: I am not a lender, a mortgage broker, or a financial advisor.

I am just a guy with a website and a passion for financial literacy. This means there are some things I simply cannot do for you:

  • I cannot give you a mortgage rate. I don't work for a bank. You have to get a Loan Estimate from a real lender.
  • I cannot approve you for a loan. I have no authority to lend money.
  • I cannot give you personalized financial advice. I don't know your income, your debts, or your credit score. It would be irresponsible for me to tell you whether you should buy a specific house.
  • I cannot help you with a loan you already have. If you have a problem with your current mortgage servicer, you need to contact them directly.

If you need personalized help, I strongly encourage you to contact a HUD-approved housing counselor or a licensed mortgage professional in your state. They have the expertise and the legal authority to help you with your specific situation.

Beware of Mortgage Scams

Speaking of lenders, I want to use this space to warn you about a massive scam that is running rampant right now.

You will see ads online that say, "Government grants for first-time homebuyers, no repayment required!" or "Get a 2% mortgage rate today!"

These are almost always scams. The government does not give out free money for houses. If an ad promises a rate that is way below the current market average, they are trying to trick you into paying upfront "processing fees" or "application fees."

If a lender asks you to pay a fee before you have even seen a Loan Estimate, run away. Block their number. Report them to the CFPB (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau).

I built SmartLoanCalc to empower you with knowledge. When you understand how the math works, you are immune to these scams. You know that a 2% rate is impossible, and you know that a real lender will never ask for an upfront fee before giving you a quote.
